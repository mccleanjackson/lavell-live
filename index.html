Key Storylines
🐻
Bear Bachmeier: Sophomore Leap
After an ankle sprain limited him in the Big 12 Championship and bowl game, Bachmeier enters 2026 fully healthy. Teammates and coaches say he's in a "flow state" carrying over from his breakout freshman campaign. He's the only true freshman in 2025 to lead an FBS program to 12 wins. This opener marks his first game in front of a LaVell Edwards crowd as a proven starter — not a surprise starter.[4,5]
🏃
LJ Martin's Senior Farewell Tour Begins
The Big 12 Offensive Player of the Year returns for his senior season having rushed for 1,305 yards and 12 TDs in 2025 — 12th nationally in yards per game (100.4). Martin has stated his goal is to finish as a Big 12 champion. This opener is the first chapter.[5]
📡
New Receiving Corps on Display
Chase Roberts (802 yds, 6 TDs), Parker Kingston (924 yds), and Carsen Ryan all exhausted eligibility and left for the NFL. BYU counters with Kyler Kasper (6-6, Oregon transfer), Jojo Phillips, Cody Hagen, and freshman Legend Glasker, who coaches say "absolutely has a chance to play this fall." The opener is the debut for this rebuilt unit.[7]
🏋️
O-Line Retooling: New Unit, Same Ambition
BYU lost three veteran starters from its 2025 offensive line. Coach TJ Woods (Big 12 Assistant Coach of the Year) rebuilds with Trevin Ostler (converted from TE to OL in spring) among the new pieces. The unit has publicly set their sights on the Joe Moore Award again.[4]
🎯
FCS vs. FBS: A Tune-Up with Purpose
Utah Tech finished 2-10 in the FCS UAC in 2025 and transitions to the Big Sky in 2026. BYU is expected to be a significant favorite. The Cougars used a 69-0 opener against Portland State in 2025 to establish rhythm — a similar outcome here would be a powerful statement entering Notre Dame prep season.[1,3]

Win Probability GAME
Real-time probability based on score, time, down & distance, and historical outcomes from thousands of comparable game situations.
Expected Points Added (EPA) EFFICIENCY
Measures each play's value by calculating expected scoring before and after. Positive EPA means the play added scoring value. Elite QBs average +0.3+ per pass attempt.
3rd Down Conversion % SITUATIONAL
% of third downs where the offense gains enough yards for a first down or scores. Teams at 50%+ dominate time of possession and points per drive.
Historical Context HISTORICAL
Win rates pulled from thousands of comparable game situations — same score differential, quarter, conference level. Helps show how safe (or precarious) a lead really is.
Havoc Rate / 4th Down Defense DEFENSE
Havoc Rate = % of plays creating a TFL, pass deflection, or turnover. 4th-down stops are high-leverage plays — each one is effectively a turnover that kills a scoring drive.

Tonight's Game — Holy War 2025

64,794 fans — a sell-out crowd — packed LaVell Edwards Stadium for the Holy War. After Bachmeier's 22-yard hurdle TD run sealed the win, BYU fans stormed the field for the third straight year. University president C. Shane Reese crowd-surfed among the students.

The Name: LaVell Edwards

LaVell Edwards (1930–2016) coached BYU for 29 seasons, going 257–101 and winning the 1984 National Championship. Inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame in 2004. Renamed in his honor after the 2000 season.

Renovation Timeline

1964
Opens as Cougar Stadium. First game draws 33,610 — a Utah state attendance record.
1982
Major expansion: end zone stands, field lowered 8 ft, track removed. Capacity surges to 65,000+.
2000
Renamed LaVell Edwards Stadium.
2018
First college football program with full-stadium NFL-grade Wi-Fi.
2024
New natural grass, LED lighting, remodeled suites. Current capacity: 62,073.
